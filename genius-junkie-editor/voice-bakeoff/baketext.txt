Now the part most tutorials skip — the maths. Your cover is one single image: back cover, plus spine, plus front cover, plus bleed. The front and back are your trim size — we're using six by nine. The spine depends on your page count and paper: white paper is zero point zero zero two two five two inches per page; cream paper is slightly thicker at zero point zero zero two five. Our notebook is one hundred and twenty white pages — that's a spine of zero point two seven inches. So the full canvas is twelve point five two inches wide by nine point two five tall. Two decimals wrong and the previewer rejects it, so let the numbers do the work.
