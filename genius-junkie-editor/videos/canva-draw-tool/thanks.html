⚠️ Do this first, before you draw anything

  1. Open the palette page and change the four blocks to your colours. They seed the document colours row, and that row is where every stroke should be picked from.
  2. Set your pen weight by typing the number, not dragging the slider — it is the only way a whole set matches.
  3. Draw in short strokes. The eraser removes whole strokes, so one long line is all-or-nothing.
  4. When a page is finished, duplicate the design and carry on in the copy.

The limit that costs people an afternoon

There is a stroke limit of 1 MB per design — not per page. Past it, Canva stops saving your drawings while still letting you draw them. The brush works, the strokes appear, nothing is being written to the file. You find out when you refresh.

Duplicating the design when a page is done gives you a fresh 1 MB every time. If you are already stuck, delete some strokes and it will save again. The stroke budget planner has a working log for this.

The one-second pause

Draw a shape, then hold still for one second before lifting. Canva replaces it with a clean version — rectangles, arrows, triangles, circles, stars, lines, hearts and diamonds. Lift straight away and nothing happens, which is why almost nobody finds it. It is the fastest way to annotate a screenshot in Canva.

About transparent backgrounds

Downloading with a transparent background is a Canva Pro feature, as is SVG. That is exactly why this kit is built around a printable sticker sheet — no transparency needed, it works on the free plan, and it is a better product to sell anyway. If you truly need die-cut stickers, that is what Pro is for.
