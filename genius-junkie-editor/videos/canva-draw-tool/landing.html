The mistake this saves you from

Canva stores every stroke as a path, and there is a limit of 1 MB of strokes per design — not per page, per design. Past it, Canva stops saving your drawings. It does not stop you drawing: the brush still works, the strokes still appear on screen, they are simply not being written to the file. So you carry on for another forty minutes, refresh, and everything past the limit is gone. It is in Canva's own help pages and almost nobody teaches it. The fix takes ten seconds: when a page is finished, duplicate the design and carry on in the copy. Each design gets a fresh 1 MB.

And two things you cannot undo

The colour is baked into a stroke when you make it — you cannot recolour it afterwards the way you would recolour a shape, so pick the colour first, every time. And the eraser removes whole strokes, not pixels, which is why you draw in short pieces: one long beautiful stroke is all-or-nothing, six short ones can be repaired.
