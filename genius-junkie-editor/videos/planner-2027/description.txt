There are two ways to send a Canva design to a buyer and they look almost
identical. One of them hands over your master file.

Canva's own words: a Template link means "anyone can view and use it as a
template" — the buyer gets their own copy. The edit link is the other one, and
Canva says it "lets people make changes without signing up or logging in to
Canva". They appear as guests, in YOUR design. There is only one of it. Twelve
buyers, one file, and the first person to delete a page deletes it for everyone.

THE SETTINGS
· Template link: Share → See all → Template link → Create template link
· The URL must contain /template. If it does not, do not send it.
· Template links are Pro, Teams, Enterprise, Education or Nonprofits only
· Deleting a template link makes the URL inactive for every buyer who has it
· Page sizes: US Letter 8.5 x 11 in AND A4 210 x 297 mm — build both
· Margin 0.5 in all round (a hole punch bites about a centimetre in)
· Month grids need SIX rows — a 31-day month starting Saturday needs six
· Export PDF Print (300 DPI), flattened — PDF Standard is 96 DPI
· If you also sell a digital version, export a SECOND file unflattened,
  because flattening kills clickable links

Sources: Canva Help Centre — "Share your design as a template link",
"Sharing designs via email or links", "Download designs as flattened PDF".
