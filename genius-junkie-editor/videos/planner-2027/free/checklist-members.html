The one that costs you the shop

Template link, not edit link.

  • Template link — ends /template. Canva: “anyone can view and use it as a template”. Each buyer gets their own copy.
  • Edit link — Canva: it “lets people make changes without signing up or logging in to Canva”. They appear as guests in your design. There is only one of it.

Twelve buyers, one master file. The first person who deletes a page deletes it for everyone — including the master you would need to fix it.

To get the right one: Share → See all (it is hidden behind that) → Create template link.

Template links are Pro, Teams, Enterprise, Education or Nonprofits only. And deleting one makes the URL inactive for every buyer who has it — so do not tidy them up.

The six checks

  1. The link says /template — not /view, not /edit. Read the URL before you paste it.
  2. Both page sizes exist — US Letter 8.5 × 11 in AND A4 210 × 297 mm. Open both.
  3. The dates are 2027 dates — 1 January 2027 is a Friday. Weekdays move every year.
  4. February has 28 days — 2027 is not a leap year.
  5. Print file is PDF Print, flattened — PDF Standard is 96 DPI. PDF Print is 300.
  6. You bought your own listing — different browser, as a stranger. The only real test.

Build settings

  • Margin 0.5 in all round. A hole punch bites about a centimetre in — half an inch clears it.
  • Month grids need six rows. A 31-day month starting on a Saturday needs six, not five.
  • Weekday names on every month, not just January. People print one month on its own.
  • Day numbers lighter than the buyer's handwriting. The numbers are furniture.
  • Resize to A4 as a copy, then check the right edge, the bottom edge, and any heading that may now wrap.

What goes in the order

  1. Printable PDF, US Letter — 300 DPI, flattened
  2. Printable PDF, A4 — the same, checked separately
  3. The template link
  4. One page of instructions — most support messages are buyers who did not know to click it
  5. If you sell a digital version: a second export, not flattened (flattening kills clickable links)
