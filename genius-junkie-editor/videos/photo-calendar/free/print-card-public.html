Your photo calendar is going to print with a white line down the edge of every single month — and you will not see it until all twelve are in the box.

Canva adds the bleed outside your page. Their words: "Canva adds 0.125 inches (3.175 mm) on all sides for products that trim or get cut", and you cannot change it — "These are fixed and are measured based on our print specifications."

So a photo you dropped in to fit the page is three millimetres short on all four sides. On screen it looks perfect. The trimmer cuts through where your photo already ended, and because no two copies are cut in exactly the same place, the sliver moves from edge to edge across the months.
