Your photo calendar is going to print with a white line down the edge of every
single month, and you will not see it until all twelve are in the box.

Canva adds the bleed OUTSIDE your page. Their words: "Canva adds 0.125 inches
(3.175 mm) on all sides for products that trim or get cut" — and you cannot
change it: "These are fixed and are measured based on our print
specifications."

So a photo you dropped in to fit the page is three millimetres short on all four
sides. On screen it looks perfect. The trimmer cuts through where your photo
already ended, and because no two copies are cut in exactly the same place, the
sliver moves from edge to edge across the months.

The fix is one drag, and it is Canva's own instruction: "resize or stretch your
background to cover them."

This builds a photo calendar properly — the pages, the photographs, the grid,
the bleed, the pixel arithmetic and the export.

THE BLEED
· Canva adds 0.125 in (3.175 mm) on all sides, outside your page. Fixed.
· File → Settings → Show print bleed. A dashed border appears.
· Canva's fix: "resize or stretch your background to cover them."
· It costs three millimetres of picture and it saves the whole calendar.

THE PIXELS
page                300 dpi page     + Canva's bleed
A4   210 × 297 mm   2480 × 3508      2565 × 3583
A3   297 × 420 mm   3508 × 4961      3583 × 5036
Letter 8.5 × 11 in  2550 × 3300      2625 × 3375

Rule of thumb: about 2,500 px on the long edge for an A4 page — BEFORE you
crop. A photo straight off a modern phone clears it. The ones that fail are the
copies: messaged, downloaded or emailed photos are compressed on the way, often
to around 1,600 px, and it is invisible on screen.

Canva's upload ceiling at the other end: under 50 MB and no more than 250
million total pixels (JPEG, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, WebP).

THE EXPORT
PDF Print — Canva: 300 dpi, "with bleed, crop marks, and color profile (RGB or
CMYK) selection option". Tick crop marks AND bleed. Not PDF Standard (96 dpi,
prints soft), and not JPG or PNG (thirteen files, no crop marks).
