Your first set, in order

  1. Open the template. Page 1 is the hero — replace the placeholder art with yours, keeping everything inside the pink band.
  2. Page 2 is the mockup. Drag your design onto the placeholder, then turn shadows on so it doesn't read as fake.
  3. Page 3 is scale. Relabel the dimension arrows with your real measurements — this is the one image where text belongs.
  4. Pages 4–7 are the information cards. Fill them in once and reuse the wording on every listing.
  5. Pages 8–10: two details and your best lifestyle shot.
  6. Delete the guides layer, then Download → JPEG at around 90%.

For the next product

Duplicate the whole design, swap the artwork and the words, and you have ten listing images in about ten minutes. Keep the card design identical across every listing — that's what makes a shop read as a brand instead of a collection, and almost no small shop bothers.
