## Description

On Etsy nobody reads anything until a picture has already earned the click.
Your listing photos are doing more work than your description, your tags and
your price put together — and there's a crop happening to your first photo
that most sellers have never noticed.

In this tutorial we build a complete ten-image listing set in Canva: the hero
that survives the crop, the mockups that make a digital file feel like an
object, the scale shot that prevents returns, and the information cards that
answer questions before they're asked. No camera, no studio.

⚠️ The one thing to know: Etsy's search results crop your FIRST photo to
**5:4 — 570 × 456 px**, taking the middle and throwing away the top and
bottom. Upload the recommended 2000 × 2000 square and the version buyers
actually see is a bad crop of it. Everything essential has to live inside the
middle 5:4 band.

SPECS MENTIONED
· Minimum 2000 px on the shortest side (below that, Etsy's zoom won't engage)
· Safe sizes: 2000 × 2000 (1:1) or 2700 × 2025 (4:3)
· Search results crop the first photo to 5:4 — 570 × 456 px
· 10 images + 1 video per listing · JPG / PNG / GIF · video 5–15 s
· Listings with fewer than five photos measurably underperform
· Export JPEG at ~90% quality

Note on file size: reported caps disagree across sources, so this video gives
the safe practice rather than a number — JPEG at ~90% keeps a 2000 px image
comfortably under a megabyte.
