Lay out a folded card the way it looks right on screen and the back of it prints
upside down. Every time. It is not a Canva bug — it is what folding is.

Fold a sheet in half and the top half turns over, so the panel that becomes the
back of your card sits at 180° to the front. Your design does not know that. It
prints the back the right way up ON THE SHEET, which is the wrong way up ON THE
CARD. Nothing warns you, because nothing in Canva knows you are going to fold it.

This is the whole card — outside, inside, fold, cut, and what to write.

THE LAYOUT
· A6 card (105 x 148 mm) folds from an A5 sheet (210 x 148 mm, landscape)
· US equivalent: a 5.5 x 4.25 in card from an 8.5 x 5.5 in sheet
· Page 1 = the OUTSIDE. Page 2 = the INSIDE. Two pages, one sheet.
· RIGHT panel = the front. LEFT panel = the back.
· The LEFT panel's content must be rotated 180° on the sheet
· The INSIDE is never rotated — it reads as one spread
· 10 mm margin all round (home printers cannot reach the edge)
· Fold guide at 105 mm, the exact centre
· Export PDF Print (300 DPI), not PDF Standard (96 DPI)

ON DOUBLE-SIDED PRINTING: your printer calls it "flip on long edge" or "flip on
short edge", and the names are not consistent between makes. This video does not
tell you which one yours needs, because it depends on your printer. Print one,
fold it, look at it — if the inside is upside down, change the flip edge and
print one more. Two sheets of paper, and faster than reasoning about it.
