Lay out a folded card the way it looks right on screen and the back of it prints upside down. Every time.

It is not a Canva bug — it is what folding is. Fold a sheet in half and the top half turns over, so the panel that becomes the back of your card sits at 180° to the front. Your design does not know that. Nothing warns you, because nothing in Canva knows you are going to fold it.

One page, with the layout actually drawn out:

  • Which panel is the front, which is the back, and which one never rotates
  • Both page sizes — A5→A6 and the US equivalent
  • The margin, the fold guide, and the export setting
  • The two-sheet test that settles your printer's flip edge
