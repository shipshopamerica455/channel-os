A business deck is the one Canva design most likely to be emailed to somebody else — and it's the one Canva is least able to hand over intact. This is the full build, plus the part nobody covers: what actually happens to your deck when the client opens it.

Three sentences from Canva's own help page, all on the same page:

"Designs downloaded as PPTX or DOCX files may look different when opened in Microsoft Powerpoint or Microsoft Word."
"You may need to make edits, or download and install the fonts you used in Canva to your device."
"Animations and embedded videos are not yet supported in Microsoft PowerPoint and Microsoft Word."

Read those together and you get the whole problem. Their machine doesn't have your font, PowerPoint substitutes it, your headlines wrap onto another line and push the body copy down, and every animation is dead. They won't tell you. They'll just think your deck looks scrappy.

The editable file is the one that changes. The file that doesn't change is the one they can't edit. There is no export that gives you both — so decide whether the deck is for reading, presenting or editing BEFORE you export it.
