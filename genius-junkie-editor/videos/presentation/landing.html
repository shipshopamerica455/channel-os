The Presentation Kit — and the reason your deck changes on the way

A business deck is the one Canva design most likely to be emailed to somebody else. It is also the one Canva is least able to hand over intact — and it says so itself, on a help page almost nobody reads.

Three sentences, all from one Canva help page

"Designs downloaded as PPTX or DOCX files may look different when opened in Microsoft Powerpoint or Microsoft Word."

"You may need to make edits, or download and install the fonts you used in Canva to your device."

"Animations and embedded videos are not yet supported in Microsoft PowerPoint and Microsoft Word."

Read them together and you have the whole problem. Their machine does not have your font, PowerPoint substitutes it, your headlines wrap onto another line and shove the body copy down, and every animation is dead. They will not tell you. They will just think your deck looks scrappy.

The editable file is the one that changes. The file that does not change is the one they cannot edit. There is no export that gives you both.
