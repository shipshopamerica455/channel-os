⚠️ The one thing to get right

Decide what the deck is for before you export it. Being read → send a PDF: layout frozen, fonts baked in, identical on any machine. Being presented → send nothing, present from Canva and share the link after. Being edited → design in a system font from the start, keep animation out entirely, and then open the exported PowerPoint yourself and look at every slide before it goes anywhere.

That last check takes two minutes and it is the difference between you finding the problem and your client finding it.

The order to do this in

  1. Set the margins first. Guides 80 px in, and nothing touches the edges.
  2. Build one content slide properly, then duplicate it. Same geometry, different words.
  3. Write headlines that state, not label. "Q3 revenue grew nineteen percent", not "Q3 results".
  4. One piece of evidence per slide. Two things fight each other and the audience picks neither.
  5. Read only the headlines before you send. Do they tell the story without you?
  6. Keep the empty shell as your template so the next deck is a fifth of the work.
