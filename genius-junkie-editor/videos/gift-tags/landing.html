Ten tags a sheet. Five cuts. Done.

The build from the tutorial, finished: print-ready gift tag sheets you can print at home tonight. No Canva plan needed — download, print, cut, write the names.

What's inside

  • 24 Christmas designs — tree, star, holly, bauble, snowflake, candy cane, present, wreath, bell, mitten, three baubles and a pine sprig, each in a classic and a kraft colourway.
  • 12 all-year designs — balloons, cake, hearts, daisy, leaf, confetti, bow, moon and stars, sun, rainbow, and a plain one that works for anything.
  • Ten tags to a sheet, 2 across × 5 down, filling the page edge to edge — so the paper's own edges are the outer edges of your tags.
  • Letter and A4 — 4.25 × 2.2 in tags on Letter, 105 × 59.4 mm on A4.
  • Pale grey cut lines on the inside lines only, and a 9 mm safe border on every tag — clear of the edge a home printer can't reach, and forgiving of a wobbly cut.
  • Write-on To and From lines and a punch-here circle at the clear end.
  • A one-page read me: print settings, the five cuts, and the full design list.

The mistake this saves you from

Print at 100% / Actual size, not "Fit to page". Fit to page shrinks the whole sheet, every tag with it, and moves the cut lines off the grid. The read me walks through it, and through the one test sheet on plain paper that catches it before you load the card.
