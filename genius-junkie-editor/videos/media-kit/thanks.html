⚠️ Do this first, before you design anything

  1. Open YouTube Studio → Analytics → last 28 days. Every number in the kit comes from that one screen.
  2. Fill in the five-numbers worksheet. Round down, and write the date range next to each figure — a rounded number with a window attached reads as measured.
  3. Then work out your three rates on the calculator. Do this before you open the template, or you will design around a number you have not decided yet.
  4. Only then fill in the Canva pages, and export with Share → Download → PDF Standard, not PDF Print.
  5. Name the file with your channel name and the month. Not "media kit final v3".

The two things that decide the price

Delivered views, not subscribers. Put your average views per video on the cover, above or instead of the subscriber count. It is the number they are actually buying.

Where your audience lives. It moves your price by two to three times for identical views. Put the country mix on page two anyway, with one line saying what it buys them — hiding it does not raise your price, it just delays the question.

Three lines to delete before you send it

  • "Rates on request" — it adds an email to the chain and says you have not decided.
  • "Negotiable" — it tells them your first number was fiction, so they open below it.
  • "Small but engaged audience" — never apologise for the size in the pitch. The numbers are on page one.

Add instead, at the bottom of the rates page: "No undisclosed ads, and nothing I have not used." It costs nothing and it tells a serious brand you will not be the creator who embarrasses them next quarter.

What's next

Re-pull the five numbers once a month — it takes ten minutes, and a kit with last quarter's figures is worse than no kit at all.
