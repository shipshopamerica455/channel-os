⚠️ Do this before you design anything

  1. Fill in the traction worksheet. Slide seven is the one they actually read, and it is easy to design once the numbers are honest.
  2. Decide the font. The deck ships in a system font on purpose — a PPTX export does not carry your font with it. If there is any chance an investor asks for an editable copy, leave it alone.
  3. Then write the slides. Every headline is a sentence, not a topic. "Clinics lose 11 hours a week to phone bookings" — not "The problem".
  4. To add a slide, duplicate an existing one and swap the words. Never start from a blank page, or the type sizes and margins drift.

The two things your exported file carries

Cropped images. Canva's own docs: a PDF export "will include the original image". Crop a revenue figure out of a screenshot and it can still be recovered with a PDF editor. Crop is a mask, not a delete.

Your speaker notes, if the Include notes checkbox is ticked. It exists so you can print your own cue cards — tick it by accident and you have emailed an investor every private note you wrote to yourself.

Both are solved by ticking Flatten PDF when you download. It converts the file to a static image, which also locks your fonts and stops anyone editing the deck in Acrobat. It makes the file bigger. Tick it anyway.

Before you send it

  • Flatten PDF ticked · Include notes unticked
  • PDF Standard (96 dpi) for email — Print (300 dpi) is for paper
  • Under 10 MB, checked after flattening
  • Filename is your company and the month, not "deck final v3"
  • Attach it. Do not make them click a link, sign in, or request access.
