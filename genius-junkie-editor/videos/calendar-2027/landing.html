The mistake this saves you from

A common year is 365 days, which is 52 weeks and one day left over. That spare day pushes every date one square to the right the following year. January 1st 2026 is a Thursday; January 1st 2027 is a Friday. So if you open last year's design and edit the year in the title, you have not made a 2027 calendar — you have made a 2026 calendar with the wrong year printed on it, and it looks completely normal. Nothing turns red. Somebody prints it, puts it on a wall, and books an appointment on the wrong day.

The one-second check

February 2027 starts on a Monday and has 28 days, and 28 days is exactly four weeks — so on a Monday-start grid it fills a perfect 4 × 7 block. Four rows, seven columns, completely full, no blank squares at either end. It is the only month of the year that does that, and it only works this year. One gap anywhere in that block and the grid is wrong, which means the other eleven are too. Every month in this kit passes it.

One thing worth knowing

Canva's automatic "add dates" feature only exists in Canva Sheets and Docs — not in a print design. In a wall calendar you are building the grid yourself with a table, which is exactly why a pre-built one saves the afternoon.
