✅ The check, before you ship anything

Open February and look at it. It should be a perfect 4 × 7 block: four rows, seven columns, completely full, no blank squares at the beginning and none at the end. February 2027 has 28 days and starts on a Monday, so on a Monday-start grid that is the only shape it can make — and it is the only month of the year that does it.

One gap anywhere in that block means the grid is wrong, and if February is wrong the other eleven are too. Every month in this kit already passes, but check it again after you restyle anything.

How to use it

  1. Pick your size — A4 or US Letter for the wall, A5 landscape for the desk. If you are selling it, include both A4 and Letter.
  2. Restyle the fonts, colours and pictures freely. Change the cover.
  3. Do not retype the numbers. Move them, resize them, recolour them — but the moment you start typing dates by hand you inherit the problem this kit exists to solve.
  4. Keep the layout identical on all twelve pages, and keep the bottom-right cell empty — it is the one cell that is blank in some months and not others.

Printing it properly

  • Export as PDF Print (300 dpi), never PDF Standard (96 dpi) — send 96 to a printer and the numbers come out soft.
  • Make two files. Bleed ON for a print shop. Bleed OFF for the home printable, because a home printer cannot print to the edge — bleed gives your buyer a white border and a cropped picture.
  • Selling it? Say the paper size in the listing. Half the complaints about printables are an A4 file printed on Letter.

⚠️ A warning about 2028

Do not reuse this file next year either. 2028 is a leap year: the shift is two days, not one, and there is a 29th of February. It is the one year where the habit bites hardest.
