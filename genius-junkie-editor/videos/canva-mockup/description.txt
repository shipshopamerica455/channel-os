ways at once — the wrong shape, twice too long, and the music is stripped out.

not contain any audio once uploaded", and "the video aspect ratio should be 2:1
or 1:2". The usual mockup is a 9:16 phone video, thirty seconds, set to music.
And Canva's mockups download as pictures, not videos — so the moving version has
to be built separately.

ETSY'S RULES (Etsy Help, "How to Add Listing Videos")
· "Your video may be 3-15 seconds long and will not contain any audio once
  uploaded to your listing."
· "The video aspect ratio should be 2:1 or 1:2."
· "While the minimum video resolution is 500px, the ideal video resolution is at
  least 1080px." Maximum file size 100 MB.
· "You can add up to 2 videos per listing."
· Listing videos "can also help improve how your listings appear in Etsy search"
  — Etsy's claim, not mine.

CANVA (Canva Help Centre)
· "Each animation can only be 10 seconds long at the maximum."
· Animated designs download "as videos or GIFs".
· Mockups: Elements → Mockups → Smartphones; Edit → Apply changes; download as
  PDF, SVG, JPG or PNG — stills only.
· Some mockups and animations are Pro — check the label on each one.
