## The trap

**Etsy's listing-video rules.** Etsy Help, "How to Add Listing Videos": *"Your
video may be 3-15 seconds long and will not contain any audio once uploaded to
your listing"* and *"the video aspect ratio should be 2:1 or 1:2."* The usual
scrolling mockup is 9:16, ~30 seconds, set to music — it fails all three.

**The fix:** build at 1080 × 2160 (exactly 1:2), keep the scroll inside Canva's
cap (*"Each animation can only be 10 seconds long at the maximum"*), hold two
seconds either side for 14 seconds total, and write every fact on screen.
