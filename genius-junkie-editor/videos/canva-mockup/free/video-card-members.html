Etsy's rules — from Etsy's own help page

From Etsy Help, "How to Add Listing Videos": "Your video may be 3-15 seconds long and will not contain any audio once uploaded to your listing." And: "The video aspect ratio should be 2:1 or 1:2."

  • Length: 3 to 15 seconds
  • Sound: none — the audio is removed on upload
  • Shape: 2:1 or 1:2 — a 9:16 phone video is neither
  • Resolution: at least 1080 px is ideal; 500 px is the minimum
  • File size: 100 MB maximum; MP4, MOV and others accepted
  • How many: up to 2 videos per listing

The usual scrolling mockup — 9:16, thirty seconds, set to music — breaks the first three at once.

Canva's side — two things nobody tells you

1. Canva's mockups are stills. Elements → Mockups → Smartphones makes a great listing photo: drag your design onto the phone, Edit to adjust, Apply changes. But it downloads as PDF, SVG, JPG or PNG — there is no video option. The scrolling version has to be built separately.

2. The ten-second cap. Canva Help: "Each animation can only be 10 seconds long at the maximum." So your scroll is ten seconds or less — which fits inside Etsy's fifteen nicely.

The build — exact numbers

video page   1080 × 2160   = exactly 1:2
screenshot   1080 wide, up to 3125 tall (Canva's height limit)

Page 1   the top of the screenshot, still        2 s
Page 2   Animate → Create an Animation, drag UP  10 s
         (hold Shift to keep it straight)
Page 3   the bottom of the screenshot, still     2 s
                                         total  14 s

Transitions off, so it reads as one shot. Download as MP4 video. If pages fly past too fast to read, the screenshot is too tall for ten seconds — take a page out, don't speed it up.

Want a phone around it? Don't animate inside a Canva mockup. Put a straight-on phone shape over the scrolling screenshot, then cover everything outside the screen with four plain rectangles in your background colour. The screenshot scrolls behind them and shows only through the screen.

Design for silence — Etsy removes the sound

  • Watch it with the sound off. What does a shopper actually learn?
  • Put the facts on screen as text: how many pages, what size, editable in Canva.
  • Put the first line on the first frame — that second is all most shoppers see.
  • Nothing that needs a voice: no "watch this", no "here's the best bit".

Seven checks before it goes on Etsy

  • The page is 1080 × 2160 — one to two
  • The video is under 15 seconds
  • The scroll is 10 seconds or less
  • Every page is readable as it passes
  • The facts are on screen, and it makes sense with the sound off
  • The first frame says what the product is
  • You made the second video too — e.g. the template opening and being edited in Canva

The fifth is the one nobody checks — because they never watch their own video silent.
