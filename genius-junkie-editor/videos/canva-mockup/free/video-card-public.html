The scrolling phone mockup most Etsy sellers make breaks Etsy's own rules three ways at once.

Etsy's help page is specific: "Your video may be 3-15 seconds long and will not contain any audio once uploaded to your listing." And "the video aspect ratio should be 2:1 or 1:2." The usual scrolling mockup is a 9:16 phone video, thirty seconds long, set to music — the wrong shape, cut short, and the music is stripped out before a single shopper sees it.

And Canva's mockups are stills — they download as pictures, not video.
