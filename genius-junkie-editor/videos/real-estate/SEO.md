## Pin comment (after publish)

The minute that matters is at 3:31. From Canva's own Content License Agreement:

"Releases are generally not obtained for Free Content, and some jurisdictions
provide legal protection against a person's image, likeness or property being
used for commercial purposes when they have not provided a release."

Read the middle of it — image, likeness OR PROPERTY. Canva names property
explicitly, property marketing is commercial use, and the same agreement puts
the job of deciding whether a release is needed, and getting it, on you.

So: no stock people on the flyer, none on the window card, and never on a
testimonial card — a stock headshot beside "sold in two weeks, wonderful
service" presents a model as a real client endorsing your business.

I am not a lawyer and you are regulated differently in every market. Sheet one
of the kit puts the clauses on a single page with the source printed, so you can
take it to your broker rather than a video:
https://geniusjunkie.net/real-estate-listing-kit/ (£4.99, or free for Template
Club members)
