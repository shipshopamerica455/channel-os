⚠️ The one thing to get right

No stock people in property marketing. Canva’s Content License Agreement says: “Releases are generally not obtained for Free Content, and some jurisdictions provide legal protection against a person’s image, likeness or property being used for commercial purposes when they have not provided a release.” Image, likeness or property — and property marketing is commercial use.

Not on the flyer, not on the window card, and never on a testimonial card. If you need a person in the picture, the person is you — photograph yourself once, properly, and use it everywhere. Sheet one has the clauses on a single page with the source printed, so you can take it straight to your broker or compliance lead.

And every photograph is of the property

If a room photographs badly the answer is to go back, not to substitute. Bright but overcast, every light on anyway, and shoot from the corner rather than the doorway — corners show a room’s real size. Sheet three is the shot list.
