⚠️ THE CLAUSE. From Canva's Content License Agreement, verbatim: "Releases are
generally not obtained for Free Content, and some jurisdictions provide legal
protection against a person's image, likeness or property being used for
commercial purposes when they have not provided a release."

Read the middle of that — image, likeness OR PROPERTY. Canva names property
explicitly, property marketing is unambiguously commercial use, and the same
agreement puts the job of working out whether a release is needed, and getting
it, on you. So: no stock people on the flyer, none on the window card, and never
on a testimonial card.

This is not legal advice. Agents are regulated differently in every market — it
is the sentence to take to your broker or compliance lead.
