## The trap

**The RSVP form belongs to whoever made it.** Google Docs Editors Help, "View &
manage form responses": *"form owners can download and delete existing
responses."* An RSVP button pointed at your Google Form sends every guest's reply
to *your* Responses tab. Built for someone else, or sold as a template, every
couple's guest list lands in your account.

**The fix is a copy, not a share.** More → Make a copy (Google's help). For a
template you sell, a Google Docs Editors **Community guide** describes the `/copy`
URL — labelled as a community guide on screen, because it is one.

Supporting facts, all from the platforms' own pages on 19 Oct 2026: the Website
template requirement for `.canva.site`; canva.link-only and no custom domain for
Mobile/Link in Bio; *"Canva Free users can have up to five live websites on the
free domain"*; the Free footer; *Resize on mobile*; the countdown via *Canva Code*
(Canva Newsroom); *Set close date* (Google Workspace).
