The RSVP form on your Canva wedding website probably belongs to you, not the couple — and every guest who replies is filling in your form.

Google's own help page is plain about it: responses are collected in the form, and "form owners can download and delete existing responses." So if you built the site for a friend, or you sell it as a template, every guest list ends up in your inbox. And sharing the form does not fix it — that only gives them access to yours.
