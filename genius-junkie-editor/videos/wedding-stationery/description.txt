"A7" is two completely different sizes, and wedding stationery is the one place
that costs real money. In America an A7 envelope is 5.25 x 7.25 inches and holds
a 5 x 7 card. Under ISO 216, A7 paper is 74 x 105 mm — smaller than a postcard.
Same two characters. Order a hundred envelopes on the wrong one and not one of
them fits.

THE MEASUREMENTS
· Invitation 5 x 7 in · details card 5 x 7 in · reply card 3.5 x 5 in
· A7 envelope (US) 5.25 x 7.25 in — this is what holds a 5 x 7 card
· A7 paper (ISO 216) 74 x 105 mm — holds nothing in this video
· Order of service / table number A5, 148 x 210 mm
· Bleed 0.125 in every side; keep type 0.25 in inside the trim

ISO 216 is definitional: each size is the one above halved across the long
side. A4 210x297 → A5 148x210 → A6 105x148 → A7 74x105.

ON POSTAGE: square envelopes, wax seals, ribbon and anything rigid are
non-machinable and carry a surcharge. No price is quoted in this video on
purpose — rates change, and the only number that matters is the one at your
counter. Weigh one complete sealed invitation before buying any stamps.
