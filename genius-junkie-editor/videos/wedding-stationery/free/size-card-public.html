“A7” is two completely different sizes, and wedding stationery is the one place that costs real money.

In America, an A7 envelope is 5.25 × 7.25 inches and holds a 5 × 7 invitation. Under ISO 216, A7 paper is 74 × 105 mm — smaller than a postcard. Same two characters. Order a hundred envelopes on the wrong one and not one of them fits.
