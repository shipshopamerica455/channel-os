⚠️ The one thing to get right

Set page sizing to Actual size — never Fit. Every print dialog defaults to some version of Fit, which shrinks your design by a few percent so it sits inside the area the printer can reach. That is enough that the card stops fitting the envelope.

Then prove it: print one sheet and measure a card with a ruler before you cut anything. An A6 card is one hundred and five millimetres wide. If it measures a hundred and one, the dialog is still scaling. Sheet two names the setting in Acrobat, Chrome, macOS and Windows.

And the reason the cards touch

On the four-up sheets the artwork runs together between the cards on purpose. Cut down that shared line and the colour reaches the edge — full bleed on a printer that cannot do full bleed. Leave the white margin round the outside of the block alone; that is the printer’s dead zone, and it is meant to be empty.
