⚠️ THE TWO THINGS YOUR PRINTER DOES

1. It cannot print to the edge. Consumer printers have a non-printable margin of
roughly three to six millimetres, so a card designed edge to edge comes out with
a white border.

2. The print dialog shrinks your design. Every dialog defaults to some version of
"Fit" — Fit to printable area, Shrink oversized pages, Scale to fit. It scales
the document down by a few percent, and that is enough that a five by seven card
no longer fits five by seven envelopes.

The fix is free: design inside a ten millimetre margin, set page sizing to ACTUAL
SIZE, and lay four A6 cards up on one A4 sheet so the trim cuts through the
artwork — which gives you full bleed on a printer that cannot do full bleed.
