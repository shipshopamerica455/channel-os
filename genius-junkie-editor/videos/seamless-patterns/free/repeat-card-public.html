The canvas edge in Canva is a crop, not a wrap. Anything you push past the edge is cut off there, and nothing appears on the other side to continue it.

Which means you can build a pattern that looks completely finished on your screen, export it, tile it, and watch a hard grid of seams appear — straight lines through your design every tile width, where the motifs stop dead at the boundary. And Canva will not show you that seam. There is no offset preview, no tile preview, no wrap mode. The design looks perfect right up until it is on fabric.
