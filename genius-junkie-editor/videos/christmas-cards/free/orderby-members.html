The rule — from Canva's own help page

The build — 5 × 7 in, portrait

  • File → Rulers and guides → Show margins. File → Settings → Show print bleed.
  • The bleed is 0.125 in (3.175 mm) on every side — Canva says it "cannot be adjusted".
  • The photo goes out to the bleed. Text and logos stay inside the margins. Nothing across the fold.
  • Landscape card? Canva's help: "the panel for the back cover needs to be inverted".

Envelopes: neither Canva's US nor UK cards page says they are included — check the product page, and order them at the same time if not.

Seven checks before you pay

  1. 5 × 7, portrait
  2. The photo fills the front, out to the bleed
  3. All the words are inside the margins
  4. The inside message leaves space to sign
  5. You read the delivery date at checkout — and it is before 11 Dec (US) / 15 Dec (UK)
  6. You went through the mock-up, inside and outside
  7. You know whether envelopes are included

The fifth is the one nobody does — the free option is already selected. Free and late is not free.
