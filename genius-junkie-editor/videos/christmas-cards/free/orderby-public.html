Canva's free shipping on printed cards is the slow one — and it can turn your Christmas cards into New Year cards.

Canva's own folded cards page lists economy delivery as free, and 11–20 days — with production on top. In the US, the post office's last date for First-Class Mail before Christmas is Thursday 17 December. Count back, and free shipping means ordering by about the tenth of November.
