## The trap

Canva's US folded cards page: Economy *"FREE"*, *"11-20 days"*; Standard *"7-12
days"*; Express *"5-5 days"*. Canva Help: production 1–2 business days (+1 after
5 PM), and *"The most accurate delivery timeframe for your order is always the one
shown at checkout."* USPS: First-Class Mail by Dec. 17. Our worked order-by dates
(cards in hand Fri 11 Dec): Economy 10 Nov, Standard 20 Nov, Express 2 Dec —
labelled as our sums on screen. UK: Royal Mail 2nd Class Fri 18 Dec, 1st Class
Mon 21 Dec.

The two Canva pages disagree (help: Economy 7–14 business days; product page:
11–20 days, and the product page itself moved from "9-20" to "11-20" overnight),
so the script uses the longer numbers and leans on "the date at checkout".
