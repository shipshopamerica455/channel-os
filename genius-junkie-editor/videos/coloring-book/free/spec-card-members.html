The one that breaks books

Minimum line weight: 0.75 pt · 0.01 in · 0.3 mm

A coloring book is nothing but lines, so this applies to every stroke in it. Canva's element line art is often 0.5 pt — under the floor. It looks perfect on screen and prints as a broken dotted line, or not at all. Work at 1 pt to give yourself the margin.

Scaling an image down scales its stroke down with it. A 1 pt line at half size is a 0.5 pt line. Resize first, then read the stroke, then fix it.

Bleed — not symmetrical

0.125 in on the top, bottom and outer edge. Nothing on the inner edge — that is the spine, and it is glued.

page height = trim height + 0.125 + 0.125 = + 0.25 in
page width  = trim width  + 0.125         = + 0.125 in

8.5 × 11 in → 8.625 × 11.25 in

Canva's bleed sits on all four sides. Do not use it — type the bled size in as your custom size.

Inside margin — by page count

  • 24–150 pages — 0.375 in
  • 151–300 — 0.5 in
  • 301–500 — 0.625 in
  • 501–700 — 0.75 in
  • 701–828 — 0.875 in

Outside margin: 0.25 in minimum without bleed, 0.375 in with it. Change the page count and the inside margin may change — go back and check it.

Exporting from Canva

  • PDF Standard — 96 DPI. Do not use.
  • PDF Print — 300 DPI. Use this.
  • Crop marks and bleed — leave unticked (you already built bleed into the page size).
  • Flatten PDF — tick it (it also embeds fonts, which Amazon requires).

Also true

  • Minimum 24 pages. Amazon rounds the page count up to an even number.
  • Images at least 300 DPI. Fonts embedded. Transparency flattened.
  • Custom trim: width 4–8.5 in, height 6–11.69 in.
  • Interior and cover are two separate uploads — lock the page count before making the cover.
  • Blank backs are a convention buyers expect (markers soak through), not an Amazon rule.

Sources: KDP Paperback Submission Guidelines; Set Trim Size, Bleed and Margins.
