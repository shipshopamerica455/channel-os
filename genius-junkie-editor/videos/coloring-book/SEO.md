THE NUMBERS, FROM AMAZON'S OWN HELP PAGES
· Minimum line weight 0.75 pt / 0.01" / 0.3 mm — work at 1 pt to be safe
· Bleed 0.125" on top, bottom and OUTER edge only — so the PDF is 0.25"
  taller but only 0.125" wider. Canva's bleed is uniform on all four sides.
· Inside margin 0.375" at 24–150 pages, 0.5" at 151–300, 0.625" at 301–500
· Outside margin minimum 0.25" without bleed, 0.375" with it
· Minimum 24 pages, rounded up to an even number
· Images at least 300 DPI — so PDF Print, not PDF Standard (96 DPI)
· Fonts embedded and transparency flattened — the Flatten PDF checkbox

Sources: KDP Paperback Submission Guidelines; Set Trim Size, Bleed and
Margins. Verified 24 September 2026.

Not covered because Amazon does not document it: single-sided printing.
Blank backing pages are a convention buyers expect, not an Amazon rule.
