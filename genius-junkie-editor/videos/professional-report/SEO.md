## Pin comment

```
Three rules that make a chart look like evidence rather than decoration:

One chart, one point — if it needs a paragraph to explain it, split it in two.
Label the bars directly instead of sending the reader off to a legend.
Use your accent colour for the number that matters and grey for everything else.

The last one does most of the work. A chart where everything is coloured is a chart that says nothing.
```
