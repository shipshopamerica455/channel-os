## Correction (1 Sep 2026)

At **7:18** the VO says a sale costs "the twenty-cent listing fee and nothing else".
Wrong. $0.20 is the LISTING fee. Each sale also carries Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee
and payment processing (US 3% + $0.25, UK 4% + £0.20, plus a 0.48% UK regulatory
fee) — about **$1.02 on a $6 printable, ~17%**. Offsite Ads adds 12–15% again.

Caught by **@di_williams** in the comments. Correction is live at the TOP of the
description on dnxwZ53IsnE, and replied to under their comment (1 Sep).

commenter instead.
