## Pin comment (after publish)

The one minute that matters is at 3:03. Canva's own photo books page says, in
their FAQ: "No, we don't offer layflat binding at this time. We do, however,
offer PUR binding using a strong bookbinding adhesive."

PUR is a good adhesive — it opens flatter than ordinary perfect binding and the
book will not fall apart. It is just not layflat, so the middle of every spread
curves into the gutter. Faces, text and horizons across that centre come back
bent. Skies and blurred backgrounds cross happily.

If you are printing somewhere other than Canva, ask them one question before you
send anything: layflat, or perfect bound? The answer changes how you lay out the
whole book.

```
Canva photo books are **not layflat**, and every double-page spread pays for it. Their own FAQ, verbatim: "No, we don't offer layflat binding at this time. We do, however, offer PUR binding using a strong bookbinding adhesive."

This matters more for an album than any other print product, because the spread — one photo running across both pages — is the entire reason people make albums instead of framing prints.

Design for the gutter, or keep faces out of the middle.
```
