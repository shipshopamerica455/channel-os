A photo album is the one design where people put the best photo right across the
middle of two pages — and it is the one design where that is the worst place to
put it. In this tutorial we build a print-ready twenty-page album in Canva, and
I show you exactly where you are allowed to put the things that matter.

⚠️ The trap, in Canva's own words. Asked whether they offer layflat binding,
Canva's photo books page answers: "No, we don't offer layflat binding at this
time. We do, however, offer PUR binding using a strong bookbinding adhesive."
PUR is a good adhesive — it opens flatter than ordinary perfect binding and the
book will not fall apart. It is simply not layflat, so the centre of every
spread curves down into the gutter. Faces, text and horizons placed there come
back bent and shadowed. Skies and blurred backgrounds cross happily.
