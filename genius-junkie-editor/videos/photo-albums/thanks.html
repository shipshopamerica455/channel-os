⚠️ The one thing to get right

Canva photo books are not layflat. Their photo books page says: “No, we don’t offer layflat binding at this time. We do, however, offer PUR binding using a strong bookbinding adhesive.” PUR opens flatter than ordinary perfect binding and the book will not fall apart — it is simply not layflat, so the centre of every spread curves into the spine.

Keep faces, text and horizons out of the pink band on sheet one. Skies, water and blurred backgrounds may cross it. If you are printing somewhere other than Canva, ask them one question first: layflat, or perfect bound? The answer changes how you lay out the whole book.

And plan twenty pages, not twelve

“Design for a front cover, back cover and from 20 to 98 inside pages.” Sheet two turns that minimum into six sections of roughly three pages each, so every page has a job instead of being padding.
