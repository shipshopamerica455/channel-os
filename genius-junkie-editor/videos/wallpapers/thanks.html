⚠️ The one thing to get right

Your phone crops the width, not the height. It scales a wallpaper until the height fills the screen and throws away whatever hangs off the sides. A 16:9 file — the 1080 × 1920 that most wallpaper templates ship at — loses about 18% of its width on a current iPhone or Galaxy, and over 20% on a Pixel. It comes back upscaled and softer too.

Design at 1440 × 3120 and every phone scales it down instead. Downscaling is the safe direction.

The order to do this in

  1. Open the phone template and build inside the green safe band — away from the clock at the top and the dock at the bottom.
  2. Bleed the background past all four edges. A visible border is a border you can watch get cropped.
  3. Delete the guide layers. Hiding them is not the same thing.
  4. Export PNG for flat colour, gradients and type; JPG only if the wallpaper is a photograph. Ignore the DPI box entirely.
  5. Check the file that lands is the size you asked for. If your phone offers to zoom it, the shape is wrong.

If you're selling these

Read the licence sheet before you list anything. Canva's terms say you "can't sell any Canva content on a standalone basis" — a stock photo exported at wallpaper size is exactly that. Your own composition, type and colour work is a product; their asset lightly edited is not. That's the rule that gets shops taken down.
