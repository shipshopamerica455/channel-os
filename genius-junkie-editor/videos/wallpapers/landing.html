Why your wallpapers keep getting cropped

Almost every wallpaper template you'll find is 1080 × 1920 — sixteen by nine, ratio 1.78. A current phone is nineteen-and-a-half by nine, ratio 2.17. Your phone will never letterbox a wallpaper: it scales the image until the height fills the screen, lets the width overhang, and crops the overhang.

That costs you about 18% of the width on an iPhone 16 Pro or a Galaxy S25, and over 20% on a Pixel 9 — and what's left is upscaled, so it's softer than you made it. Canva's own wallpaper page still recommends "1024 x 768 and a screen ratio of 4:3" for desktop, which is CRT-era advice.
