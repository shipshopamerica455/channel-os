There are two PDF exports in Canva and they look equally reasonable. One of them makes a file your buyer never finishes downloading.

Canva's own help pages are explicit: PDF Standard is “for multi-page and text-heavy documents” and for “sharing through email”. PDF Print is “for printing”, and “the only file type that saves professional print quality at 300 DPI”. An ebook is read on a screen — 300 DPI buys nothing a screen can show.
