The checkbox this saves you from

Underneath the file-type list in Canva's download menu there's a checkbox called Flatten PDF. It sounds like housekeeping. What it does is bake every layer into a flat image and strip every hyperlink out of the document — sponsor links, product links, your entire contents page. And the file still looks completely perfect, so you send it, and you find out weeks later, if you find out at all. The read-me walks the whole export, including the two other delivery routes the same file feeds.
