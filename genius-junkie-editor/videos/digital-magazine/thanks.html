⚠️ The export — the part that matters

  1. Share → Download → PDF Standard, not PDF Print. Standard is roughly 2 MB and opens on a phone; Print is roughly 20 MB and is for paper.
  2. Leave the Flatten PDF checkbox unticked. Flatten bakes every layer into a flat image and strips every hyperlink out of the document — and the file still looks perfect afterwards, which is why nobody catches it.
  3. Open your own exported file and click three links before you send it to anyone.

Two more routes from the same file

  • Publish as a website — same design, Share → Publish as a website. Canva gives you a free .canva.site address and every link still works; on the free plan you can keep up to five live at once. A PDF is frozen the second somebody downloads it; a website you can just fix.
  • A true page-turn flipbook needs a viewer outside Canva — Canva has no page-turn on any plan. Feed the viewer exactly the PDF you just made. Most are monthly subscriptions and most host your file, so the day you stop paying the link stops working.
