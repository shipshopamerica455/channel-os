## The trap

**Hyperlinks survive exactly one export, and Canva's own font fix destroys it.**

From the Canva Help Centre, "Add hyperlinks": *"Hyperlinks will only work if you
download your design as a PDF (except flattened PDFs)"*, and *"Image formats
like JPG and PNG do not support hyperlinks regardless of the image source"* —
the page also rules out MP4s and posting to social media.

The second half is what makes it a trap rather than a fact. From "Download
designs as flattened PDF": flattening *"converts your design with multiple
layers into one"* and *"ensures fonts display accurately and secures your
design"*. So the fix Canva offers for the most common newsletter complaint —
fonts rendering wrong on somebody else's machine — is also the one setting that
silently kills every link in the file. **Neither the export dialog nor the file
says so.**

**The second trap** is the quieter one: PDF Standard is *"96 dpi"*, PDF Print is
*"300 dpi, with bleed, crop marks, and color profile"*. School, church and
community newsletters all get printed, and 96 dpi prints soft.

Verified 10 October 2026.
