Every link in your Canva newsletter is probably dead, and nothing told you.

Canva's own help page says it plainly: "Hyperlinks will only work if you download your design as a PDF (except flattened PDFs)", and "Image formats like JPG and PNG do not support hyperlinks regardless of the image source". So if you exported a JPG and pasted it into an email — which is what most people do — every link in it is a picture of a link.

And here is the part that catches careful people. If your fonts came out wrong on somebody else's machine and you ticked flatten to fix them, which is Canva's own suggestion, you just killed every link in the file a second time. Flattening merges every layer into one image, and your links were layers.
