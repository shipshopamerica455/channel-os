Most clickable digital invitations stop being clickable the moment you send them.

Canva's own help page says why: "Hyperlinks will not work on PNGs, JPGs, MP4s, etc., or when you post your design on social media." And saving the invitation as a picture or a video and dropping it into the group chat is exactly how most people send one — so every guest gets a lovely envelope that does nothing when they tap it.

The fix is to build it as a small website, link the envelope to the next page, publish it on Canva's free address, and send the link — not the picture.
