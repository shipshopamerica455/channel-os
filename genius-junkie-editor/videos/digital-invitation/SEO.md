## The trap

Canva Help, "Add hyperlinks": *"Hyperlinks will not work on PNGs, JPGs, MP4s, etc.,
or when you post your design on social media."* The usual way to send a digital
invitation — save it as a picture or a video, drop it into the group chat — is
exactly that case. **The fix:** build it as a Canva website (envelope → card →
details, linked with *Pages in this document*), publish it free, send the LINK.
A file for email or a printer: the standard PDF, not the flattened one.
