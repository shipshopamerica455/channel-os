Canva will not let you make a tall infographic. Their maximum design size is 8000 × 3125 pixels, and that is per axis — so the second number is your height ceiling.

Three thousand one hundred and twenty five pixels, for a format whose whole point is that it scrolls. Type a taller number into the custom size box and the fields turn red and refuse you — with no warning text, no suggestion, and no offer to scale it for you. Most people discover this halfway through, delete a section, and ship something thinner than they meant.

There is a way round it, and it is arithmetic.
