## The trap

**Canva's maximum design size is 8000 × 3125 px, per axis** (minimum 40 × 40).
The height ceiling is the one that bites, because an infographic is the single
format that is inherently tall. Canva Help Centre, "Resize designs and size
limits": if the custom size fields turn red "it means the values are less than
or exceed the size limits" — and that is the entirety of the explanation.

**The fix is arithmetic.** The same page says a design downloads as a PNG "from
0.5 up to 3.125 times its size". So 900 × 6000 is built at **450 × 3000** and
exported at **2×**. The ceiling is on the canvas, never on the export.

**The second trap** is Canva's own doing: `canva.com/create/infographics`
recommends 663 × 2000 for a blog, 1200 × 628 for Facebook and 1104 × 736 for
LinkedIn. One tall column and two landscapes — not variations of each other.
