⚠️ The one thing to get right

Share and Assign are not the same button. A share link — edit, comment or view — points every student at the same design. Paste that into Google Classroom and thirty students are editing one file.

Assign — top corner of the editor, or Share → Assign — gives every student their own copy, and their copy comes back to you.

Two expiries worth writing down: the assignment link stays active for 30 days; the assignment code dies after 4 hours. Generate codes in the lesson. Use the link for anything planned.

The order to do this in

  1. Check eligibility first — the sheet in this pack lists exactly who qualifies and who doesn't. Don't build a term on an account you can't keep.
  2. Verify, then invite your students from your settings. Consent letters out for anyone under 13.
  3. Type this week's content into a shell. Don't redesign the page — that's the whole time saving.
  4. Hand it out with Assign.
  5. Collect on Classwork and filter by status: Not submitted · To review · Done · Resubmission requested.

If you're not eligible

Tutors, homeschool educators who aren't licensed or employed by a school, lecturers, public librarians, parents, teachers not currently in post — that's Canva's list, not ours. The shells still work, and Share → Template link gives every learner their own copy on a free account: "anyone with the link can use this design as a template." You don't get Classwork, statuses or submissions — work comes back however it already does.
