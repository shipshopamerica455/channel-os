## What's in the template

`#2b2118`, accent `#b5432e`. Centered name + identity line, STARTERS (3) /
MAINS (5) / DESSERTS (2) with bold dish lines, prices tucked at line end,
**no currency symbols, no trailing zeros** (matches the S07 pricing pass in
the video), italic grey descriptions, QR placeholder box + label bottom-left,
specials footer. Everything editable text.
