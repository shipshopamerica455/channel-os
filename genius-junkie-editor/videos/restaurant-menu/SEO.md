## Pin comment

```
The rule most menus break: great photos sell food, average photos kill it. If you have professional shots of your actual dishes, use two or three per page. If all you have is phone snaps under kitchen lighting, use NONE — text-only menus read as more upscale anyway, which is why fine-dining menus are pure typography.

And never use stock photos of food you don't serve. Customers notice, and review sites remember.
```
