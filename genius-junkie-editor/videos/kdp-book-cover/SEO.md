## Description

Shoppers judge your book in three seconds at thumbnail size — and Amazon
rejects covers that fail its measurements. In this tutorial we design a
complete KDP paperback cover in Canva: front, spine and back on one
wraparound canvas with the exact spine maths Amazon uses, the barcode zone
you must leave clear, and the export that passes the Launch Previewer first
try. Plus the matching Kindle ebook cover in five extra minutes.

THE MATHS (save this):
• Cover width = 0.125 + trim + spine + trim + 0.125 (inches)
• Spine: white paper = pages × 0.002252″ · cream = pages × 0.0025″
• 6×9, 120 white pages → 12.52″ × 9.25″ (spine 0.27″)
• Spine text only at 79+ pages, with 0.0625″ clearance each side
• Barcode zone: ~2″ × 1.2″, bottom-right of the back cover
• Ebook cover: 2560 × 1600 px, JPEG

```
The corner you must leave alone: Amazon prints its barcode in the bottom-right of the BACK cover — a 2 × 1.2 inch block — and KDP drops it on top of whatever is already there.

So keep that corner clear of text and busy artwork. It is not optional and it is not negotiable, and it is the single most common reason a beautiful cover comes back looking broken.
```
